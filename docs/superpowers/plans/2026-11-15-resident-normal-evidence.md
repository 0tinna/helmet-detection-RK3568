## Global Constraints

- Normal entry remains the genuine owned wrapper, with --verify-resident-ui only; no smoke/auto-demo dispatch, APIstart/WiFi writes or fabricated ACK/render/visibility.
- Shared coordinator /userdata/helmet-validation/resident-state and fixed v4 root; all new evidence under /userdata/helmet-validation. No NPU execution by implementer/reviewer; root serializes one actual task.
- Short total observation at least120 seconds and conservative hidden interval at least30; long formal interval entirely hidden and at least1800 by last.request_started-first.response_finished. FPS uses upper denominator last.response_finished-first.request_started; fixed10detected/output target stays separate.
- Status/process/ACK every1s, preview every5s, formal max gaps3s/10s and ACK wait5s. Formal starts after client terminal and matched real hidden ACK, ends before re-show. All failures/signals retained.
- Credentials/token never exported. Raw body bytes/text consistency, exact complete inventory, source/helper/release identities and actual terminal/lease/private-file states are required. Emergency cleanup cannot satisfy UI action gates.
- Preserve production sources, normal helpers, old raw runs, bundles and frozen manuals. Physical focus/touch/power, labelled quality, continuous display, direct SDK release and leak-free claims remain separate.

## Review Focus

- Accepted intent without current Qt ACK cannot pass visibility, hide or restored-page gates.
- Client exit0 or disappeared HTTP cannot pass terminal state while manager/service/Qt or inherited lease is live.
- Failure-counter growth, missing raw files/body mismatch, delayed observation or late owned signal must fail.
- New show/generation invalidates old images; only actual displayed packet binding and fresh backend completion can pass.
- Wrong selected model/source/profile or unbound keyboard plan must fail before formal acceptance.
